SAMPLE 01ENGLISH

Oral Book Review: The Kite Runner

Author: Khaled Hosseini

Read the complete English sample

A kite is supposed to symbolise joy, freedom, and childhood memories, right? But what if I told you that one kite-flying competition could change the lives of two friends forever?

The Kite Runner—don't get confused by the name. It's not just a novel about flying kites. It's a story about friendship, betrayal, and the journey towards redemption.

Written by Khaled Hosseini, it's a historical fiction novel set mainly in Afghanistan. The story revolves around two childhood friends, Hassan and Amir, who come from different social backgrounds but share a common love for kite flying.

However, one incident changes their lives forever. What could that incident be? Don't worry, I won't rob you of the wonderful experience of discovering it for yourself!

What makes this book extra special to me is that it was the first novel I ever read. It showed me that stories can be much more than entertainment; they can leave a lasting impact on us.

The most powerful aspect of this novel is its portrayal of friendship. It shows how loyalty can bring people closer, while betrayal and silence can tear them apart.

Another important theme is redemption. Imagine getting a chance to correct a mistake you made in the past. Would you take it? Would you have the courage to face your past and make things right? In this story, Amir gets a chance to confront his past and seek redemption. And that makes you wonder: is it ever too late to make things right?

I would recommend The Kite Runner to anyone who enjoys emotional stories that make you think beyond the pages. It can be heartbreaking at times, but it also makes you reflect on friendship, forgiveness, and the choices we make.

For me, this book taught me that we cannot always change what has happened, but we can choose how we respond to it.

And if my first novel could leave such a lasting impression on me, I believe it might do the same for you.

So, give The Kite Runner a chance. You might pick it up for the story, but you may put it down with a completely different perspective on friendship and life...

SAMPLE 02SUBJECT · CYBERSECURITY

ExpanSe — Brief (Cyber Security)

Read the complete subject sample

Introduction

In ExpanSe, sensors send readings such as crack width or expansion to the server. Since these readings may be used as proof later, we need to make sure the readings are not manipulated.

Threat Model

An attacker could change a reading, send an old reading, or modify the database. The attacker may want to tamper with evidence in a dispute about property or money.

HMAC

Each sensor and server share a secret key. The sensor uses the key to create an HMAC. The server also creates the HMAC again and checks if they match.

HMAC gives integrity and authenticity, but does not provide non-repudiation, freshness, or confidentiality.

Replay Protection

An attacker can send a previous reading for which the HMAC will match. To stop this, we add a counter or nonce to every reading. The server checks whether the counter is new or an old one is being replayed.

The counter must be included inside the HMAC-protected data so that it cannot be changed.

Hash Chain

The stored readings are connected using a hash chain:

h₀ = H(h₋₁ || payload₀)

Every record depends on the previous one. If someone manipulates old records, the hash no longer matches the new record, so the change can be detected.

However, if someone has access to the database, they could recalculate the whole chain. So, the head hash should be stored somewhere outside the database.

Conclusion

Overall, my part in ExpanSe is to make sensor readings secure, trustworthy, traceable, and difficult to tamper with.
